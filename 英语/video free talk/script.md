It’s not far from the city center, but it feels like another world. With green forests, old streets, and a calm sea shore, it’s the perfect place to relax and learn a little history in weekends and escape the hustle and bustle in modern cities.

When you arrive on Qi’ao Island, the first sense you will feel is the fresh air and peaceful surroundings. The island has one of the largest mangrove forests in Guangdong. Mangroves are a kind of special trees that grow in saltwater near the coast. They protect the shore and give homes to birds, fish, and crabs. There’s a long wooden walkway through the mangroves—great for walking or biking, especially in the early morning or late afternoon when the light is soft and golden. It has a very beautiful nature.

Qi’ao Island also has an interesting history. Back in 1833, local villagers stood up against foreign traders who were trying to bring opium into China. The people of Qi’ao won this small but important fight—and later, they used money paid by the British to build a special street called “White Stone Street.” You can still walk on this historic street today. The stones under your feet tell a story of courage and pride.

The island is also the hometown to Su Zhaozheng, a key leader in China’s early labor movement. He also helped organize workers during the famous Canton-Hong Kong Strike in the 1920s. Today, his former home on the island is a memorial museum where you can learn about his life and contributions to modern China.

As college students, we spend most of my time studying or using our phone. But on Qi’ao Island, I finally slow down. I enjoy riding a bike along the coast, sitting by the water, or just listening to the wind and birds singing. It’s simple, quiet, and real.

If you’re ever in Zhuhai, I really recommend visiting Qi’ao Island. Whether you love nature, history, or just need a break from busy life—you’ll find something special here. And you can access the island easily by the bridge connecting Zhuhai city and QIao island.
