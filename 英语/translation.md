In the long history of vehicle development, new energy vehicles are reshaping the pattern of global world's vehicle industry powerfully, and the China's new energy vehicle industry has made a great and impressive transformation from the chaser to the leader.

 The rapid development of  China's new energy vehicle industry can't be done without the continuous pushing of technical innovation. In recent years, Chinese companies have obtained significant breakthrough in several key fields. In the aspect of battery, manufactures like CATL lead the world in  energy density and charging speed, providing stronger power and  more convenient charging experiences. Advancement in battery efficiency have the performance of vehicle further enhanced. At the same time, the intelligentizing development  also highlights the Chinese electrical vehicles. Companies including BYD, NIO and XPENG have broken through in the autonomous driving technology and intelligent vehicle-mounted systems, which makes driving easier and safer.

Beyond this, new technologies involving active suspension systems, smart light system and BYD's DiSus-Z intelligent suspension system, has constant popped and facilitated the industry stepping into the "Four-Electric Era"(electric drive, battery, electric control, and charging), significantly improving the controllability of the vehicle and ride comfort.

It's worth mentioning that the appear of EREV and ultra-fast charging technology  has solved the key problems of the China's new energy vehicle development. EREV is based on electric power .When the battery is low,  the gasoline engine can charge it. With the ultra-fast charging technology, "range anxiety" has  

become the history. In a long period of time, range anxiety has been constraining the sales of pure electric vehicles, and its effective solution undoubtedly pours powerful impetus into China's new energy vehicle market.

Nowadays, China's new energy vehicles have stood in front of the global development of the industry. Through constantly innovating, optimizing industrial structure and positively facing challenges, China is expected to further consolidate the leadership in new energy vehicle field, leading the industry to a more intelligent and sustainable future.
